**Moka Pot Simulator** is a physics-based web application that lets you explore the process of brewing coffee with a moka pot. Whether you're a coffee enthusiast or just curious about the brewing process, this simulator provides a realistic and interactive experience.

- **Realistic Physics:** Simulates water flow, pressure, and temperature.
- **Customizable Variables:** Adjust grind size, water amount, heat, and more.
- **Educational:** Learn how each variable affects the final brew.
- **Accessible:** Runs directly in your browser—no installation required.
